Trip Details

What Awaits You in Punta Cana

Come for the experience. Stay for the connections. ✨ The only requirement? You're single.

This 5-day, 4-night Punta Cana escape is designed for social, open-minded travelers primarily in their mid-20s to mid-40s looking for real connections, unforgettable memories, and a little bit of adventure during cuffing season 👀

You'll stay at a 5-star, adults-only, all-inclusive beachfront resort with curated social experiences including a welcome mixer, private catamaran party, group nightlife, and more — all intentionally designed to bring people together naturally.

All meals and unlimited drinks are included at the resort — breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner. Group transportation handles everything, so all you have to do is show up and enjoy.

⚠️ We currently have over 400 people on the waitlist and only 60 spots will be available. Once spots are gone, they're gone.

Day-by-Day

Your 5-Day Journey

Arrival & All-White Welcome Mixer

Your trip begins the moment you land in Punta Cana. Enjoy seamless round-trip airport transportation to the resort, where you'll check into your 5-star all-inclusive stay and settle into vacation mode.

At 6pm, start the weekend strong with our signature Mix & Bingo night — music, drinks, appetizers, and good vibes as the sun sets over the Caribbean.

Dress code: White out and dress to impress. You never know who you're going to meet 👀

🛬 Airport Transfer 🏨 5★ Resort Check-in 🎉 All-White Welcome Mixer 🎰 Mix & Bingo Night

Adventure Day With Travel & LIV ⚡️

You're really going to get to know each other TODAY!

This is NOT your typical tourist excursion. This is the day where strangers turn into real friendships.

We're taking the group to one of Punta Cana's top adventure parks for a full day packed with adrenaline, chaos, laughs, competition, and unforgettable moments together.

Think:

🏎️ Off-road buggy adventures
🌴 Ziplining through tropical landscapes
⛰️ Scenic chairlift views
🎉 Interactive group games designed to get everyone actually connecting — including Blindfolded Ball Game, Design Your Tribe and more collaborative activities that will really have the entire group laughing, bonding, and stepping out of their comfort zones together 👀

This isn't just an ATV excursion where everyone disappears into their own groups. This experience is intentionally designed to bring the entire group together in a fun, high-energy environment where connections happen naturally all day long.

A traditional Dominican buffet lunch is also included!

⚡️ Limited availability due to transportation & excursion capacity.

🏎️ Off-Road Buggies 🌴 Ziplining ⛰️ Chairlift Views 🎉 Group Bonding Games 🍽️ Dominican Buffet Lunch

Sip, Sail & Mingle — Private Catamaran 🍊

Get ready for the highlight of the trip — a private catamaran experience exclusively for our group. Step aboard and cruise through crystal-clear waters with music, drinks, and nonstop energy setting the tone for the day.

We'll take you beyond the resort with snorkeling stops, a visit to a private beach, and time to relax in a natural pool. Enjoy an open bar with cocktails flowing, plus a fresh barbecue lunch prepared by a private chef on board.

🍊 Dress code: Orange! Wear your best orange outfit — this is the moment to stand out.

⛵ Private Catamaran 🤿 Snorkeling 🏖️ Private Beach 🍹 Open Bar 🍖 BBQ Lunch On Board

Free Day & Farewell Dinner

Today is yours — do whatever your heart desires. Maybe you met someone you can see yourself with, maybe you made new friends. Explore the beautiful town, relax by the pool, or soak up the last bit of Caribbean sun.

Just remember, you have free will today and you can do whatever you want... within accordance to the law, of course! 😛

In the evening, we gather one last time for a farewell group dinner together. For the partiers, we'll head to a local bar/club (at your own expense) to truly soak up the connections we've all made.

🌴 Free Day to Explore 🍷 Farewell Group Dinner 🎶 Optional Nightlife

Departure — Until Next Time! ✈️

All good things come to an end — but the memories don't. After a few unforgettable days, it's time to check out and head back home with new connections, stories, and moments that will stay with you long after the trip.

Round-trip transportation will take you back to the airport, making your departure smooth and stress-free. And while this trip may be over, the group chat — and the connections you made — are just getting started.
