About This Trip

Day-by-Day

Your 5-Day Journey

Arrival, Check-In & Welcome Mixer 🥂

Arrive in Punta Cana and check in to your 5-star all-inclusive beachfront resort. Take in the views, unpack, and get settled — this is your home for the next 5 days.

Tonight we kick things off right with an exclusive All-White Welcome Mixer at 6pm. Meet your travel crew, sip on cocktails, and vibe out — the connections start here.

✈️ Airport Transfer Included 🏨 Resort Check-In 🤍 All-White Welcome Mixer @ 6pm 🍹 Welcome Drinks

Adventure Day With Travel & LIV ⚡️

Get ready for a full day of adrenaline! We're taking it to the jungle and beyond with a packed adventure itinerary — ATV riding through the mud, horseback riding through lush trails, zip-lining over the canopy, and a chairlift with panoramic views.

Then cap the night off with an optional visit to Coco Bongo — Punta Cana's hottest nightclub with live performances, open bar, and 1,000+ capacity of pure energy.

🏍️ ATV Riding 🐴 Horseback Riding 🌿 Zip-lining 🎪 Chairlift 🎉 Coco Bongo (Optional)

Sip, Sail & Mingle 🌊🍾

We are turning it up on the waters! Today is our exclusive private catamaran boat party — think open bar, music, snorkeling stops in crystal-clear Caribbean water, and non-stop vibes with the whole crew.

Dress code: Orange. Wear your best orange outfit and bring the energy — this is the most iconic day of the trip.

⛵ Private Catamaran 🍊 Orange Outfit Day 🤿 Snorkeling Stop 🍹 Open Bar On Board 🎶 Music & Vibes

Free Day & Farewell Dinner 🌅

Today is yours. Sleep in, hit the beach, relax by the pool with unlimited all-inclusive drinks and food, book a spa treatment — do whatever calls to you. You've earned it.

In the evening, we come together one last time for our Farewell Dinner — a curated group dinner to celebrate the connections made, the memories built, and the friendships that'll last long after this trip.

🏖️ Free Beach & Pool Day ♾️ All-Inclusive Dining 💆 Spa Available 🍽️ Group Farewell Dinner

Time to Head Home 🛫

This is not goodbye — it's see you later. Enjoy your final morning at the resort, grab breakfast, and soak in those last Caribbean views before your airport transfer.

You're leaving with a full heart, a camera roll full of memories, and a group of people who started as strangers and ended as family. See you on the next one. ✨

🌅 Final Morning at Resort 🚗 Airport Transfer Included ✈️ Safe Travels
